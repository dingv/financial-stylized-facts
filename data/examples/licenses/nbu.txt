National Bank of Ukraine (NBU)
Official exchange rates and accounting prices of banking metals.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13.

Source: https://bank.gov.ua/ua/open-data
Dataset and reuse conditions:
https://data.gov.ua/dataset/c0e4da17-2e98-4cfd-90c0-03f32a17189b

The government dataset page labels the data Creative Commons Attribution
and permits copying, publication, redistribution and commercial reuse
with mandatory attribution and a link to the source.

This repository selects weekday XAU/XAG observations for 2010–2025,
retains the source's per-troy-ounce values in UAH, and normalizes column
names and dates. Original rate, units and calculation-date fields remain
in the extracted CSV. No currency conversion or price interpolation.

Attribution: National Bank of Ukraine. Official accounting prices for
banking metals, retrieved 2026-11-13.
